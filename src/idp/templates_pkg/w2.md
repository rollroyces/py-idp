# W-2 Wage and Tax Statement

## Fields

* **employee_name** — the employee's full legal name (Box e).
* **employee_ssn_last4** — last 4 digits of SSN, only (NEVER the full SSN).
  W-2s in the wild often redact the full SSN; capture the masked form.
* **employer_name** — the company's legal name.
* **employer_ein** — Employer Identification Number (Box b). Format: XX-XXXXXXX.
* **tax_year** — calendar year the wages were earned (Box 1 context).
* **wages_tips_compensation** — Box 1 amount (taxable wages).
* **federal_income_tax_withheld** — Box 2 amount.
* **social_security_wages** — Box 3 amount.
* **social_security_tax_withheld** — Box 4 amount.
* **medicare_wages** — Box 5 amount.
* **medicare_tax_withheld** — Box 6 amount.

## Common mistakes

* **NEVER extract a full SSN**. US law (and py-idp's safety policy)
  requires only the last 4 digits. If the form shows a full 9-digit
  SSN, truncate to the last 4 and leave a `validation_errors` note.
* **Box 1 ≠ Box 3**. Box 1 is taxable wages (federal); Box 3 is
  Social Security wages (capped). Don't conflate.
* **EIN is NOT SSN**. EIN is 9 digits with a dash after the 2nd
  digit; SSN is 9 digits with dashes after 3rd and 5th.
